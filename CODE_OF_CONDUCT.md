# Code of Conduct

## Standard

`webaudio-kit` is a small technical project. Keep discussion focused,
respectful, and useful.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different levels of experience with Web Audio, React, and TypeScript.
- Keep feedback specific to the code, documentation, or project direction.
- Assume good intent, but correct unsafe or misleading audio claims quickly.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeatedly pushing medical, audiology, or hearing-test claims for this
  library.
- Any conduct that would reasonably make contributors feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, and other
contributions that violate this code. Maintainers may also block contributors
from participating in the project.
